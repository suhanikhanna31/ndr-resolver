A human resolves a reply. It is embedded (phones and pincodes redacted) and shown to the model as a few-shot example next time a similar reply arrives.

RULES, IN ORDER005
  1. Opt-out or abuse always goes to a human.
  2. Unclear intent always goes to a human.
  3. Already received goes to a human: possible fake delivery, check proof of delivery.
  4. Confidence floor. 0.75 for reversible actions, 0.90 for RTO, which cannot be undone.
  5. Reschedule needs a date inside the window and fewer than the max attempts.
  6. Address change needs a pincode and is never auto-pushed. A human verifies.
  7. Phone change must be a valid Indian mobile, checked in code.
  8. Any failure (API down, bad schema, no tool call) escalates. It never guesses.
